Title: The Great Disconnection: A Complete History
Author: Dr. Sarah Chen, PhD
Body:
The Great Disconnection occurred approximately 15 years ago, though exact dating has become increasingly difficult as most calibrated timekeeping devices failed during the initial electromagnetic pulse.

In a single moment, the world as humanity knew it ceased to exist. Electricity grids collapsed across every continent. Communication networks went silent.

The first 48 hours were chaos. Major cities descended into anarchy as food supplies were raided and fires burned unchecked.

Those who survived were forced to adapt quickly. Communities formed around remaining resources. Knowledge that had been taken for granted suddenly became the difference between life and death.

This article compiles the most comprehensive timeline of events available from fragmented records and survivor testimonies.
